AniList Follow Back Finder: Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This policy covers version 1.2.0 of the independent Chrome extension AniList Follow Back Finder. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AniList.

What the extension does

At your direction, the extension requests public AniList users or public activity authors, checks public bios for configured phrases, records matching accounts, and optionally opens their profiles in background tabs. It does not follow accounts or send messages.

Data processed and saved

Requests return public account IDs, usernames, bios, avatar image addresses, and activity authors. The extension processes those bios locally. It stores settings, checked account IDs, pagination progress, matching bio excerpts, usernames, avatar URLs, discovery/opening timestamps, opening status, scheduling state, counters, and diagnostic logs in this browser's extension storage. Logs can contain matching usernames, page numbers, timestamps, and errors. Complete nonmatching bios are not saved. It does not read your general browsing history, AniList cookies, login credentials, private messages, or private lists. Its own profile-opening history is recorded as described above.

Network requests and sharing

Public data queries are sent over HTTPS to graphql.anilist.co without authentication or cookies. AniList receives normal network metadata, including your IP address, the page-query parameters, and the time of each request. Displaying avatar images contacts the HTTPS image hosts supplied by AniList. Opening a profile loads AniList normally in your browser, under your ordinary AniList session and AniList's own policies. No saved history, settings, or diagnostic logs are uploaded to the publisher, an analytics provider, or an advertising service. There is no publisher-operated backend, analytics, advertising, sale of data, or remote executable code.

Your controls and retention

Scanning starts only when you click Start or Scan now. You can stop or pause scanning, use Preview mode, clear account history, clear logs, export history/settings, or import a backup. Logs retain at most 300 events; checked profile IDs retain up to 10,000 per source. Account history and settings persist until you clear them or uninstall. Clearing history resets scan cursors and checked IDs; settings and scheduling state remain unless you stop/change them. Data is stored in local extension storage rather than Chrome Storage Sync. Exported files remain wherever you save them, including any cloud-synced download folder; deleting extension data does not delete those files. Imports stay local. Uninstalling removes the extension's local storage.

Permissions

Storage saves your settings and results. Alarms support scheduled scans when the dashboard is closed. Access to graphql.anilist.co enables read-only public-data queries. The extension creates profile tabs without requesting broad access to your browser's tabs.

Limited use and changes

Data is used only for the user-facing discovery, scheduling, history, backup, and troubleshooting features described here, consistent with Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. A later release that changes data practices will update this policy and disclose material changes in the extension.
